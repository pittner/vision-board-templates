# Free printable vision board templates

Six print-ready vision board sheets you can send to a printer straight from the browser.
**No signup, no e-mail address, no watermark.** Every sheet is plain SVG under an MIT licence,
so you can edit it, remix it, or put it in a workshop pack you charge money for.

**Print them here: https://pittner.github.io/vision-board-templates/**

| Sheet | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Six Life Zones** | The classic. Six areas of life, one image each, plus the sentence that ties them together. |
| **One Goal, Loud** | A single goal that deserves the whole page: one image, three reasons, one next action. |
| **The 12-Month Wheel** | One word per month around one word for the year. Fills up as the year goes. |
| **Now / Next** | An honest picture of today next to the chosen picture of later, and the habit between them. |
| **The 90-Day Sprint** | Three months, three proofs. A vision board that has a deadline attached. |
| **Pocket Cards** | Four wallet-sized cards per page. For the mirror, the wallet, and two to give away. |

## Why these and not another PDF pack

Most printable vision board templates stop at "paste pictures here". These do not.
Every sheet ends with two things a mood board never has: **a sentence in the present tense**
and **one physical next action with a date on it**. That is the difference between a board you
look at and a board that moves something.

## How to use

1. Pick the sheet that matches your moment. A new year wants the wheel. A breakup or a job
   change wants Now / Next. One obsession wants One Goal, Loud.
2. Press **Print this sheet**. Choose *Save as PDF* in the print dialog if you want a file.
   That produces a vector PDF at the exact paper size, which beats any PDF we could host.
3. Fill the picture areas first and the writing lines second. Images decide what you want,
   words decide what you do.
4. Put it where you already look every day. The fridge and the inside of a wardrobe door beat a drawer.

A4 and US Letter are both supported: switch the paper toggle at the top before printing.

## Downloads

Each sheet can also be saved as **SVG** (vector, editable in Figma / Illustrator / Inkscape)
or **PNG** (1654x2339 px, A4 at 200 dpi) directly from the page. No server involved: the files
are generated in your browser from the inline SVG.

## Prefer it generated for you?

These sheets are deliberately empty, because a printed board is something you fill by hand.
If you would rather answer a few questions and have the images made for you, the same people
who published these templates run a generator:

- **[Free browser maker, no signup](https://pittner.github.io/free-vision-board-maker/)** - build a digital board and a phone wallpaper in your browser
- **[48 free AI image prompts](https://pittner.github.io/vision-board-prompts/)** - MIT licensed prompts for Midjourney, DALL-E and friends
- **[visionboard.bemooore.com](https://visionboard.bemooore.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=templates_readme)** - write your goals, get a finished board, a 1080x1920 lock-screen wallpaper and one concrete next step. First preview is free and needs no e-mail; the full-resolution unlock is a one-off EUR 8.99.

## Technical

One static HTML file, inline SVG, no build step, no dependencies, no tracking cookies.
`build.py` regenerates `index.html` from the sheet definitions if you want to change the geometry.
