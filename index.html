How to use a printed vision board

  1. Pick the sheet that matches your moment. A new year wants the wheel, a breakup or a job change wants Now / Next, one obsession wants One Goal, Loud.
  2. Print it. Plain paper is fine, 120 g feels better under glue.
  3. Fill the picture areas first and the writing lines second. Images decide what you want, words decide what you do.
  4. Write a real next action with a real date on it. A board without a next step is a mood board.
  5. Put it where you already look every day. The fridge and the inside of a wardrobe door beat a drawer.

Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. The sheets are MIT licensed. Print them, sell the workshop you run with them, put them in a client pack. Attribution is welcome and not required.

Can I get a PDF?

Use the print button and choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. That produces a vector PDF at the exact paper size, which is better than any PDF we could host.

Will it print correctly on US Letter?

Switch the paper toggle at the top before printing. The sheets are drawn at A4 proportions and are scaled to fit Letter with a small margin, so nothing is cut off.

Where is the source?

On GitHub. The whole page is one static HTML file with inline SVG and no build step.
